Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Beans Android app (com.monkeylabs.beans)

The short version

  • Your data never reaches us. Beans has no server and no accounts. We, the developer, cannot see your transactions, your messages or anything else you keep in the app.
  • Beans reads your SMS only to find bank, card, wallet and UPI transaction messages. Personal messages are skipped, and one-time passwords (OTPs) are never stored.
  • Everything is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database.
  • Backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, an encrypted copy goes to your own Google Drive, locked with a passphrase only you know.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.

1. Who we are

Beans is developed and published by Rohit Dhiman, an individual developer trading as MonkeyLab and based in Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir, India ("we", "us"). Beans is built so that we never receive your personal data. Everything described below happens on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you choose to back up.

2. SMS access

Beans's core feature is building your expense record automatically from transaction SMS. With your permission, Android lets the app:

  • Read new SMS as they arrive (RECEIVE_SMS), so a payment shows up in Beans within seconds.
  • Read your existing SMS inbox (READ_SMS), once during setup, to import past transactions (by default the last 6 months), and again only if you ask it to re-import.

How the app handles your messages:

  • Each message is checked on your phone. Only messages from bank, card, wallet and UPI senders that describe a transaction are kept.
  • Personal, promotional and other non-financial messages are ignored and not stored.
  • One-time passwords (OTPs) are never stored.
  • No SMS is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

SMS access is optional. You can decline it, or turn it off at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Beans › Permissions. You can still add expenses and income by hand.

The following is stored only in Beans's private storage on your device. We cannot see or access it.

4. Optional backup to your Google Drive

Like chat apps that back up to Google Drive, Beans can keep a copy of your data in your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. This is off until you turn it on in Settings.

  • Encrypted on your phone first. The backup is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase you choose, before it is uploaded. Neither we nor Google can read it. If you forget the passphrase, the backup can't be opened, and we can't recover it for you.
  • Stored in your Drive, not ours. The file sits in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It counts towards your Drive storage. We never receive it.
  • Limited access. Beans asks Google only for access to its own hidden folder (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Your Google account. To connect Drive, Android's Google sign-in shows you which account to use. Beans uses it only to reach that folder and doesn't send your account details to us.
  • Turn it off at any time. You can stop backups and delete the backup in Settings. You can also remove Beans's access in your Google Account › Security › Third-party apps with account access, and delete the hidden data in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps.

Google's handling of your Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy.

5. Exporting your data

You can export your transactions to a file (for example CSV). The file is created on your phone, and you decide where it goes. An exported file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

6. Other permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to tell you about a new transaction and let you confirm or change its category. Notifications can show the amount and merchant. You can hide their content on the lock screen in Android Settings.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): if you turn on app lock, Android checks your fingerprint or face. Beans only receives a yes or no from Android and never gets your biometric data.
  • Internet: used only for the optional Google Drive backup.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share your personal data. We don't have it.
  • We don't show ads. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't run any server for your data. Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for the app. Backup happens only through the encrypted Drive backup you choose.
  • We don't use your data for credit or lending decisions, or pass it to anyone who does.

8. Security

Your on-phone data is kept in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. The key is protected by your phone's Android Keystore. You can add a PIN or biometric app lock. Drive backups are encrypted on your phone with your passphrase, then sent to Google over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and its software up to date.

9. Deleting your data

Because we hold none of your data, you are always in control of deleting it:

  • On your phone: uninstall Beans, or clear its storage in Android Settings › Apps › Beans › Storage. This deletes it permanently.
  • Your Drive backup, if you made one: delete it in Beans › Settings › Backup, or in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Beans › Delete hidden app data.

Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete your data page.

10. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance. Because Beans keeps your data only on your phone and in your own Drive, you can see, correct, export and delete all of it yourself in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off SMS access or backup, or by uninstalling the app. If you have a question or complaint, contact us below. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

Beans is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Beans handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change would ever send any of your data off your phone in a new way, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent first.
